Get the Facts

Election Logistics

Q: Is the April 21 special election still happening?

A: Yes. Despite ongoing legal appeals in the Virginia Supreme Court, the election remains scheduled for April 21. We are moving forward to ensure Virginians have their say.

Q: When does early voting begin?

A: Early voting starts March 7, 2026. You can vote in person at your local registrar’s office or request a mail-in ballot until April 14.

Understanding the Amendment

Q: Is this a permanent change to our Constitution?

A: No. This is a temporary, one-time adjustment. The authority to update these maps expires on October 31, 2030, at which point the standard post-census process resumes.

Q: Why can’t we wait until the 2030 Census?

A: Waiting until 2030 leaves Virginians with outdated, unrepresentative maps for two more election cycles. This amendment fixes the imbalance now for the 2026 and 2028 elections.

Addressing the Opposition

Q: Is this a partisan power grab?

A: No. This is about accountability. The proposed maps actually increase the number of competitive "swing" districts, forcing both parties to listen to voters rather than relying on safe seats.

Q: Will this impact minority representation?

A: Yes—positively. The amendment mandates compliance with the federal Voting Rights Act, ensuring that communities of color are protected and not diluted.
